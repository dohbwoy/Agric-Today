Agriculture is more than what we grow. It is the people, businesses, innovations and communities that make food systems work.

**AgricToday**, presented by GreenMinds Media, is a space for useful knowledge and fresh perspectives from across the agricultural world.

We will share stories about farming, agribusiness, markets, climate-smart practices, technology and the people building new opportunities across Africa.

Have a story, insight or innovation to share? Visit our [contributor page](/admin/) to get involved.
